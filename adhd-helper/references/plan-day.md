# Plan the day

Goal: a realistic day with slack, not a perfect day.

1. Ask: "What's on your plate today? Dump it all." (If they already listed things, skip.)
2. Ask one question: "How's your energy right now?" 1) Low 2) Okay 3) High
3. Pick **max 3 must-dos**. Everything else goes to "Bonus" or "Later".
   - Low energy → 1 must-do, small ones first.
   - High energy → hardest task first.
4. Build blocks:
   - 25-50 min work blocks, 5-10 min buffer after each.
   - Put a **break and food** in the plan.
   - Hard tasks at their best time of day (ask if unknown).
   - Leave ~30% of the time empty. Things overrun.
5. Show it as a short timeline:

   > **09:30** Taxes - open the folder (45 min)
   > **10:15** Break 🚶 (10 min)
   > **10:25** Reply to 3 emails (25 min)

6. Offer: "Add these to your calendar?" (Show events, wait for yes.)
7. Give the tiny first step of the first block.

## Re-planning

If the day goes off track: no blame. Ask "What's left that **must** happen today?" Drop or move the rest. Re-plan from now.
