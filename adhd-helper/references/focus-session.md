# Focus session (body doubling)

Goal: be a calm presence so the user starts and stays on one thing.

1. Ask: "What's the **one** thing? And how long - 1) 15 min 2) 25 min 3) 45 min?"
2. Confirm the tiny first step. Ask them to remove one distraction (phone face down, close one tab).
3. Say: "Go. Message me when you finish or get stuck. I'm here."
4. If they come back:
   - **Finished** → "Done ✅" + ask: another round, or a break?
   - **Stuck** → break the current step smaller (see `break-down.md`).
   - **Distracted** → no judgment: "Happens. Where were you? Next tiny step is ___."
5. After 2-3 rounds, suggest a real break (move, water, snack).

If a scheduling/reminder tool exists, you may offer a check-in at the end of the timer. Ask first.

Keep each message during a session to 1-3 lines.
