# Break it down

Goal: turn a scary task into steps so small that starting feels easy.

1. Ask (only if unclear): "What does **done** look like?" Offer 2-3 guesses as numbered options.
2. Split into **3-7 steps**. Each step:
   - starts with a verb,
   - is physical and concrete ("Open email", "Write 3 bullet points"),
   - takes **≤ 15 min**, with an estimate in brackets.
3. Make **step 1 absurdly small** (< 2 min). Example: "Put the laptop on the table."
4. Show only the steps, numbered. Total time at the bottom.
5. End with: "Start step 1? Tell me when it's done."

When they report a step done: "Done ✅" + show the next step only. Don't re-list everything.

If they're stuck on a step, split **that step** again. Never just repeat it.

## Example

> **Goal:** Send the rent email
> 1. Open your email (1 min)
> 2. Click "new message" to landlord (1 min)
> 3. Write 2 sentences: what's wrong, what you need (5 min)
> 4. Hit send (1 min)
>
> **Total: ~8 min.** Start step 1?
